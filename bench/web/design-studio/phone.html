      About this preview
      Designs are made by a finite local pattern generator: solid, stripes, checker, dots, waves, grid, named colors or #RRGGBB, quoted text, scale, rotation and seed. It does not draw objects or understand photos. The idea helper is a local, rule-based assistant, not an AI service. Tracking is experimental 2D translation: it does not estimate depth or wrap curved surfaces, and clear, glossy or plain objects often cannot be tracked. Imported PNG, JPEG or WebP images stay on this device. If tracking is uncertain, the outline and overlay disappear.
